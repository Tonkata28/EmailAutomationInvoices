# Privacy Policy — Email Invoice Automation

## Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how this email invoice automation (“the Automation”) handles information when a user connects their email account and enables invoice forwarding.

## What the Automation does

The Automation helps users route invoice emails to their chosen accountant or accounting contact.

When enabled, the Automation may:

- Monitor incoming email messages.
- Determine whether an email appears to contain an invoice.
- Download invoice-related attachments from qualifying emails.
- Forward invoice files or invoice-related email content to the accountant email address provided by the user.
- Apply labels or folders in the user’s email account to help organize processed messages.

## Information processed

To perform the workflow, the Automation may process:

- Email sender, recipient, subject line, body, timestamps, and labels.
- Email attachments, including invoice files.
- Invoice-related details visible in the email or attachment, such as vendor name, invoice number, amount, due date, and billing information.
- The accountant email address provided by the user.
- Processing metadata, such as whether an email was classified as an invoice and which label was applied.

## How information is used

Information is used only to provide the invoice automation workflow, including identifying invoice emails, forwarding relevant invoice files to the designated accountant, and organizing the user’s mailbox with labels.

The Automation does not sell personal information.

## Email access

The Automation requires access to the user’s connected email account so it can read incoming messages, access invoice attachments, send or forward relevant invoice files, and apply labels.

Users should only connect email accounts they are authorized to use.

## Sharing with accountants

When an email is identified as an invoice, the Automation may send the invoice file or relevant invoice content to the accountant email address configured by the user.

Users are responsible for ensuring that the accountant email address is accurate and that they have permission to share invoice information with that recipient.

## Data retention

The Automation may retain processing records only as needed to operate, troubleshoot, improve, or audit the workflow. Email messages and invoice files may also remain in the user’s email account and in the accountant’s mailbox after forwarding.

If the Automation stores copies of attachments or processing logs, they should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the invoice routing purpose.

## Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures should be used to protect email data and invoice attachments from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss.

Because email forwarding involves transmission to another mailbox, users should confirm that their accountant’s email address and mailbox are secure.

## User controls

Users may be able to:

- Disconnect their email account.
- Disable the Automation.
- Change the accountant email address.
- Remove or change labels applied by the Automation.
- Delete forwarded or stored invoice files where technically available.
- Request deletion of processing records, subject to legal or operational requirements.

## Third-party services

The Automation may rely on third-party services, including email providers and automation infrastructure, to read emails, process attachments, apply labels, and send forwarded messages.

Those services may process data according to their own terms and privacy policies.

## Children’s privacy

The Automation is not intended for use by children and is designed for business or administrative invoice processing.

## Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

## Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or the Automation’s handling of email and invoice data, contact the operator of the Automation.
